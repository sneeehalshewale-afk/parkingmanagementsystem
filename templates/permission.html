🚦 Permission & Safety Rules

🔒 Permissions

  • 👨‍🎓 Students & Teachers: Park freely in available slots.
  • 🛡️ College Staff: Monitor parking & guide traffic.
  • 🚶 Visitors: Allowed only if permitted by staff.

📜 Rules

  • 🅿️ Park only in free slots; don’t block others.
  • 👮 Follow staff or security directions.
  • 🚨 Avoid reserved/emergency zones.
  • ⏰ Remove vehicles after college hours.
  • ⚠️ Report accidents or damage immediately.
  • ✍️ Keep vehicle details updated in the system.

🛡️ Safety Rules

  • 🎯 Always wear a seatbelt inside campus.
  • 🏎️ Follow campus speed limits (20 km/h).
  • 🚶 Watch for pedestrians while driving.
  • 📵 No phone use while driving.
  • 🛑 Follow traffic signs & markings.
  • ⚡ Drive safely and responsibly at all times.

💡 This system tracks check-in/check-out for monitoring purposes only. No pre-booking required. Stay safe and follow the rules!
